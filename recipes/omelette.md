# Cheese Omelette

## Ingredients
- 3 eggs
- 50 g cheese
- 30 ml milk
- 1 tbsp butter
- Salt

## Instructions
1. Whisk the eggs with milk and salt.
2. Melt the butter in a pan over medium heat.
3. Pour in the eggs and cook for 2 minutes.
4. Add grated cheese and fold the omelette in half.
5. Cook for 1 more minute and serve hot.
