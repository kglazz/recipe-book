# Pancakes

## Ingredients
- 2 eggs
- 250 ml milk
- 150 g flour
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 tbsp butter
- Pinch of salt

## Instructions
1. Whisk eggs, milk, sugar and salt.
2. Add flour and mix until smooth.
3. Heat a buttered pan over medium heat.
4. Pour a thin layer of batter and cook 1-2 minutes per side.
5. Serve with jam or sour cream.
