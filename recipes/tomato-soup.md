# Tomato Soup

## Ingredients
- 6 ripe tomatoes
- 1 onion
- 2 garlic cloves
- 500 ml vegetable broth
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions
1. Chop the onion and garlic and fry in olive oil for 3 minutes.
2. Add chopped tomatoes and cook for 5 minutes.
3. Pour in the broth and simmer for 15 minutes.
4. Blend until smooth.
5. Season with salt and pepper and serve hot.
